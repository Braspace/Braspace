# BS-058 — Lunar south-pole gallery handoff

The five English cards in [`LUNAR_GALLERY.json`](../data/LUNAR_GALLERY.json) link directly to their original LROC pages. They cover Shackleton rim, Connecting Ridge, Malapert Massif, and a broader polar view. Image IDs are included only when the page states an ID; a page number is not a PDS product ID.

These website images remain on LROC's site. The [LROC terms](https://lroc.im-ldi.com/about/terms) distinguish copyrighted Featured Images from public-domain PDS products and specify the educational credit “NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University.” The gallery contains no copied images or thumbnails. Engineering can render each card as text plus a normal link, including in a low-bandwidth or no-JavaScript page. A thumbnail needs separate product-level rights and provenance review before use.

All five are flagged `illustrative_only`. Oblique camera imagery and regional mosaics do not establish the precise feature-center coordinates in [`LUNAR_SOUTH_POLE_SITES.md`](../data/LUNAR_SOUTH_POLE_SITES.md). In particular, a spacecraft sub-point must never be presented as a landing candidate coordinate. This content is ready for human review and UI integration; no gallery UI has yet been implemented.

Original pages: [South Pole](https://lroc.im-ldi.com/images/1276), [Connecting Ridge](https://lroc.im-ldi.com/images/1247), [Malapert](https://lroc.im-ldi.com/images/1294), [polar mosaic](https://lroc.im-ldi.com/images/237), [Islands in the Dark](https://lroc.im-ldi.com/images/1105).
