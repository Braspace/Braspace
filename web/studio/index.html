Why can two sites differ?

Local terrain blocks low-angle Sun and Earth rays differently. Sunlight fraction alone cannot guarantee battery adequacy or a radio link. See the same-window table below.

How were the numbers computed?

POST /api/compare evaluates stored grid samples at the exact UTC start and duration. For every row, the data status and scientific status accompany fractions and maximum continuous gaps. No value appears for a site without an AVAILABLE series.

Science is not the same as engineering

OBSERVATION / SCIENCE

Where can we see the Sun and Earth?

LOLA terrain elevation, local horizon, SPICE Sun/Earth look angles and the stored visibility grid support geometric questions. Input resolution and epoch matter.

SYSTEM / ENGINEERING

Does a specific lander survive?

Power budgets, load, battery, reserve, thermal and radio systems need explicit spacecraft assumptions. Geometric Earth visibility is not proof of an RF link.
