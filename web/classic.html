Sites with an ingested horizon/ephemeris series are evaluated from that data by the Go API. Sites without one fall back to the synthetic training pattern, and the result says so.

Crew consumables apply to computed-series sites. Rates are placeholders (0.84 kg O2, 1.76 kg food per person per day) pending a check against NASA BVAD; the crew is assumed aboard during the delay.

Power is an assumed constant whenever the synthetic Sun is visible. Communication evaluates Earth geometric visibility only, not an RF link.
