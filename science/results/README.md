## Validation

`validate_vs_horizons.py` compared 12 epochs per site and body against JPL Horizons. Horizons used a topocentric site, the `MEAN_ME` frame, and airless apparent AZ-EL. Results are in `validation_horizons.json`:

- **Sun:** difference below 0.000001°.
- **Earth:** difference up to 0.00003° in elevation.

This validates the **ephemeris geometry only**.

## Not validated yet

- **Terrain horizon:** no independent reference. Next step: compare sunlit % against the NASA GSFC PGDA illumination maps (Mazarico et al. 2011).
- **Coordinates:** these are region **feature centres** from the catalog, not optimal or actual landing points. Malapert's summit (5,163 m) is ~5 km from the point used.
- **DEM resolution:** 80 m/px. Near-field slopes are smoothed, and nearby boulders or rims are missed. Nobile Rim 1's 11.6° horizon should be checked against a 5 m/px PGDA DEM.
- **Sun and Earth as points:** no limb darkening, power model or antenna pattern.

Do not present these numbers as NASA-certified.
